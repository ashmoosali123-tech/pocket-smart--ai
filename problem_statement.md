# Problem Statement

## Background
Digital payments make spending effortless but invisible. Small daily purchases (food, rides, subscriptions) add up, and most people only notice at month end.

## Problem
1. Manual expense logging is tedious, so people stop doing it.
2. Existing budgeting advice is generic and not tied to the person's real spending.
3. Traditional apps show numbers but do not explain them or suggest what to change.

## Target Users
- College students on a monthly allowance
- Early-career professionals
- Anyone who wants a lightweight, private expense tracker

## Objective
Build an AI assistant that (a) classifies expenses automatically, (b) predicts upcoming spending, and (c) explains and advises in natural language.

## Success Criteria
- Categorization accuracy of at least 85%
- Add an expense in under 10 seconds
- Chat answers grounded in the user's own data
