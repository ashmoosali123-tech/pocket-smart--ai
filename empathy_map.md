| Says | Thinks |
|------|--------|
| "My allowance is gone already?" | "Tracking every expense is a chore." |
| "I'll start budgeting next month." | "Am I spending too much on food?" |

| Does | Feels |
|------|-------|
| Checks UPI history when broke | Anxious near month end |

## Pains
Manual entry, no insight, no personalised advice, fear of judgement.

## Gains
Effortless tracking, clear picture of habits, small actionable tips, savings confidence.
